SVV can help assess potential fluid responsiveness in appropriately selected patients.

SVV may be unreliable with arrhythmias, spontaneous breathing, low tidal volume, altered chest compliance, and other conditions.

Clinical safety

This calculator is an educational decision-support aid, not a validated medical device or treatment-ordering tool. Reference ranges are approximate adult values and vary by patient, measurement method, and clinical context. Correlate with the patient's examination, trends, laboratory results, waveforms, and facility protocols. Medication and fluid decisions require clinical judgment and appropriate orders.
